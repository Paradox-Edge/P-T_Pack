[b]P-T "Split"[/b]

A minor modification to the well-established Runasimi High-Resolution LIDAR, reversing half of the scanning frequency 180 degrees, resulting in a split-scan. It trades full detection for more consistent overlap.


[b]P-T "Triangle"[/b]

Three conical scanners aligned to produce good overlap at the cost of long-range detection.


[b]P-T "Shell"[/b]

Despite its simple construction and low resolution scan, it provides an effective, high performance sweep via an interpolative scanning method.


[b]P-T "Radix"[/b]

The "Radix" scanner recycles infrared light in a straight-through scanning system, resulting in extreme radial scan coverage as the light bounces back and forth to and from the ship several times.


[b]P-T "Comb"[/b]

A novel take on a novel concept, the love child between sweeping and phase scanners, liquid crystals result in a beautiful chromatic array of scanning, ready to be interpreted by your poor, ailing on-board computer.


[b]P-T "Pulse"[/b]

A sidegrade to the CL-144PLA Phased LIDAR Array. By inserting a prism and swapping the processor, the ghosting mess becomes a much cleaner, sharper image, offering much higher long-range precision scanning at the cost of instantaneous proximity scanning due to frequent gaps.
